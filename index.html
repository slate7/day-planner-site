day-planner

A personal scheduling tool, run by and for one person.

day-planner reads its owner's Google Calendar, works out which hours are genuinely free, and proposes a plan for the next day: tasks placed in real gaps, coursework spread across the days before it's due, and leftover space put toward longer-term goals. Nothing is written to the calendar until the owner approves the proposal.

What it does

  • Reads events from the owner's calendars to find free time.
  • Tracks assignment deadlines and how much work is left on each.
  • Mirrors those assignments into a Google Tasks list so they can be checked off.
  • Writes approved time blocks to one dedicated calendar, Planned (AI), and never to any other.

Who it's for

This is not a product and has no users beyond its author. It is a personal automation that runs on a schedule against a single Google account. It is published here only because Google requires an OAuth consent screen to link to a homepage and policies.
